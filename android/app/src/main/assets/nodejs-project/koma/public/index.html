    Picture

    In full screen the picture is fitted onto the whole screen, whatever shape the television is: the black bands of the file are cut away, and if the show is squarer than the screen it is widened a little rather than cut into. Turn this off to keep the frame exactly as it was made, with the artwork of the show around it.

    Playback

    At a season’s final episode, Koma offers the next related season or movie without leaving full screen. Turn this on to continue after the countdown instead of choosing Yes.

    A card plays its trailer while you point at it — with the pointer on a computer, with the focus on a television — and an episode in the list plays itself. The previews are silent.

    Appearance

    Turn this off for a lighter, still interface on older phones, computers and televisions. It removes transitions, smooth scrolling, blur effects, spotlight autoplay and ambient video previews; playback controls keep working.

    App

    The X hides the window instead of quitting — the stream keeps going, and the tray icon brings it back or quits properly.

    Account sign-ins stay on this device and are not included in backup files.
